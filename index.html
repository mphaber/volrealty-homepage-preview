Our Story

Knoxville Through and Through

Volunteer Realty did not begin as a strategic expansion or a franchise rollout. It began at a kitchen table in 1962, with a simple belief that if you treat people right, show up when it counts, and do your work with care, a business will stand the test of time.

That belief has carried us through six decades of serving East Tennessee. We have watched neighborhoods rise, families put down roots, and entire districts reinvent themselves, and one thing has not wavered: a commitment to doing business the right way.
